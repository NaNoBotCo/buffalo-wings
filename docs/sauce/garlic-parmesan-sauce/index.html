a sauceGarlic parmesan sauce

also: garlic parm · garlic parmesan

'Garlic parm' at the counter. Half the shops mean a creamy sauce, half mean butter and a dry cheese shake, and the menu rarely says which.

A pale, garlicky wing coating with parmesan in it. Two versions share the name: a creamy emulsified sauce built on oil and egg yolk with parmesan powder, sold bottled; and a kitchen mix of melted butter, minced garlic and grated parmesan tossed over the wings and finished with more cheese and parsley. Neither has heat in it. Both leave the wing wet and the fingers cheesy.

The story Cited · wp-wingstop

Garlic parm belongs to the sauce-board era. As the market for chicken wings expanded, restaurants began creating and using sauces beyond Buffalo, and a wing menu turned into a list — Wingstop carries thirteen wing sauces, some running only for a marketing window, and sells ranch, blue cheese, honey mustard and cheese sauce as dips beside them.

Inference — garlic parm is the wing answer to garlic bread, which is crusty bread topped with garlic and butter and sometimes cheese, then toasted. A kitchen that already had butter, garlic and a tub of grated parmesan did not need a new ingredient to add a flavour to the board.

The sauce splits the same way lemon pepper does: dry shake or wet sauce. The bottled version settles the argument by emulsifying, so the cheese stays suspended and the sauce keeps on a shelf.

How it is done Tradition

Melt butter with minced or roasted garlic, off the heat so the garlic does not brown. Toss the fried wings in it, then shower grated parmesan and chopped parsley over the top while the butter is still wet enough to hold them. The bottled sauce skips the melting: it pours cold and clings, because oil and egg yolk hold it together.

Today Harvested · fdc-kinders-garlic-parmesan

On the board at every wing chain and most bars. As of 16 September 2026 the USDA label panel for Kinder's Garlic Parmesan Wing Sauce & Dip reads 120 mg sodium and 1 g sugar in a 1-tablespoon serving — the lowest sodium of any bottled wing sauce measured for this set — with soybean oil first and roasted garlic puree second. No Scoville number is published, and the label names no chile.
