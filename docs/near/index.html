Thin spots

No place here carries lgbtq+ welcoming, all flats or all drums, closes when sold out, closed. That says nothing about America's wing counters — it says where our reading stopped. What would earn it: lgbtq+ welcoming — the business's own statement, a press list whose framing says the businesses in it are LGBTQ-owned, or a public directory (an LGBTQ chamber of commerce, a Pride business list, OpenStreetMap lgbtq=welcome); all flats or all drums — the menu; closes when sold out — the business's own site or a dated press piece; closed — a dated press piece or the business's own notice. Thin so far: woman-owned (2), naked, no breading (1), smoked (3), bone-in only (3), wing night (1), halal (1), cash only (2). Every directory we read is listed on where all of this came from. A business that wants a tag it has earned can say so on its own site or in any of those directories, and we read it there.
