The one number anybody publishes

1 of 22 bottles here carries a Scoville figure. It is the cayenne pepper sauce under most Buffalo wings in America, and it lands below a jalapeño. Scoville heat units come from a lab assay of capsaicin in dried material, and the article behind the scale says the number moves with seed, climate, soil and method. Every figure below is somebody's published measurement, lined up on one axis.
