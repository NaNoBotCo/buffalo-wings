a person · NYDrew Cerza

also: the Wing King

Founded the National Buffalo Wing Festival in 2002 and runs it. A marketing man by trade, he took the idea from a film that invented a Buffalo wing festival that did not exist, and built the one that does.

The story Cited · wp-national-buffalo-wing-festival

The 2001 film Osmosis Jones has Bill Murray's character planning a trip to a National Chicken Wing Festival in Buffalo. No such thing existed. Buffalo News columnist Donn Esmonde asked in print why not. Cerza, then working in food promotion, answered the column. "I loved wings and my hometown of Buffalo, so, I decided that it was time" for a festival, he told Visit Buffalo Niagara. The first one ran at Sahlen Field in 2002.

The festival's own accounting, as of the page read in September 2026, puts it at roughly $200,000 raised for local charities, about three million wings served with a hundred sauce varieties, and over 500,000 visitors across its run. Cerza was named Buffalo Citizen of the Year in 2003 and Tourism Executive of the Year in 2011. In 2007 he beat Bobby Flay in a wing Throwdown filmed at the Anchor Bar. In 2011 the festival put its own founder into the Hall of Flame, alongside Bill Murray.

In 2013 he inducted John Young.

Cerza does not pretend to neutrality on the arguments, which makes him useful to quote rather than to paraphrase. On ranch: "It's sacrilegious. Putting ranch dressing on wings is like putting ketchup on filet mignon." On whether he would use a sauce other than Frank's: "Are you serious??? Never! I have Frank's Red Hot running through my veins." And on the city: "I love Buffalo. It's a great city, with awesome people and the best regional food in the country."

Today Cited · web-buffalowing-wing-king

The festival runs Labor Day weekend and returned to Sahlen Field in 2025 after five years at Highmark Stadium. Cerza is president and CEO of Just Wing It Productions, the agency his 1994 firm RMI Promotions became.
