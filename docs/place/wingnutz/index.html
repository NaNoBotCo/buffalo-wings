a joint · NYWingnutz

also: Wingnutz Bar & Grill · WingNutz Bar and Grill · Wingnutz Buffalo

A sports bar on Millersport Highway in Williamsville, run by Ed and Alicia Wrazen, whose wings ran for thirteen years out of a Knights of Columbus hall before they had a restaurant. A podcast episode in 2021 turned a local following into an international one. The wings are large, crisp and sauced in mixes the couple make themselves.

The story Cited · web-wingnutz-about

The Wrazens started at home in 2005. Buffalo Rising's Mark Lazzara, writing in August 2019, traced "a 13-year journey" from home catering through cooking jobs to the Knights of Columbus hall on Kenmore Avenue, and reported that Yelp had counted Wingnutz "the best Chicken Wing Spot in the State of New York." His description of the product: "smothered in their homemade secret sauce, while so crisp."

The house's own account of why they started is short: "Our background begins a long time ago (2005), in a kitchen far away (our house). As our story begins, we were unemployed." They had been eating wings across Buffalo and had not found what they wanted.

In 2021 the hosts of Pardon My Take, a sports podcast the house says draws more than 750,000 listeners an episode, ate the Wrazens' wings and published the verdict as a title: "We Found The Best Wings In The World." Step Out Buffalo's Brett Llenos Smith, writing in January 2024, dates the full sports bar to after that: "After appearing on the popular Pardon My Take podcast in 2021, Ed and Alicia Wrazen turned to A.J. Giordano to take their Wingnutz restaurant to scale." The Millersport Highway room took over the former Santora's Pizza Pub & Grill space.

How it is done Cited · web-wingnutz-about

Fried, then sauced in house mixes. The signature is "sweet with a touch of tang"; the hot is "hot, yet bursting with flavor (and addictive!)". Step Out Buffalo's word for the wings themselves is "larger, juicier and crispier."

Today Cited · web-wingnutz-about

Three rooms in the Buffalo area as of September 2026: Millersport Highway in Williamsville, Genesee Street in Cheektowaga inside the Buffalo Airport Hotel, and a Lakeland location. The Millersport room posts Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday 11am to 10pm and Friday and Saturday 11am to 12:30am; Monday and Tuesday are not posted anywhere this project could read.
