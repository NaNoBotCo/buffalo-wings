WING COUNTRY — AGENT BRIEF (shared by every drafting agent)
==========================================================
Repo: this directory. Every path below is relative to it.
Corpus: a scratch folder of Wikipedia articles as plain text, one per file, each with its
        URL and fetch date at the top. The brief that launches an agent names the folder;
        tools/fetch_wiki.py rebuilds it.

READ FIRST, IN THIS ORDER
  1. AUTHORING.txt              the record spec and the full cast list
  2. schema/node.schema.json    what a record must look like
  3. data/nodes/style/buffalo.json · data/nodes/term/wing.json · data/nodes/place/anchor-bar.json
     the three exemplars, written by hand. Match their depth, their voice and their
     provenance discipline. A style record carries what / story / how / today and 8-12 kin.
  4. data/vocab/*.json          regions, facets, tags, recognizers, types

WHAT YOU DO
  Write the records in YOUR batch (below), one JSON file per record at
  data/nodes/<type>/<id>.json. Nothing else. Do not touch tools/, do not touch
  data/sources/sources.json, do not edit another agent's records.

SOURCES
  Cite only ids that exist in data/sources/sources.json, OR add your own to
  data/sources/new-<your-batch-name>.json in the same shape:
    {"sources":[{"id":"s:web-slug","kind":"web","title":"...","publisher":"...",
                 "url":"https://...","accessed":"2026-09-16"}]}
  build.py merges those in. Use s:wp-<slug> ids for the corpus articles (the slug is the
  filename without .txt). For anything not in the corpus, use WebFetch and register the
  page as s:web-<slug> with its real title, publisher, URL and the date you read it.
  NEVER invent a source, a URL, an ISBN, a date, a price, a quote, a menu item or a death.

PROVENANCE, WHICH IS THE POINT OF THE PROJECT
  provenance.default.tier plus per-field overrides. Tiers:
    cited      a named source in sources[] backs this
    harvested  a tool fetched it from an open dataset (OSM, USDA)
    tradition  general knowledge of the tradition; hedge it in the prose
    inference  this project reasoning from the above; say so in the prose
    field      somebody stood there (nothing carries this yet)
  In text.story mark each stretch inline: plain prose = cited; *Tradition holds —* ;
  *Inference —*. A fact you cannot source goes in hedged or stays out.
  Set needs_verification: true and confidence: "medium"/"low" where you are unsure.

VOICE (house rules; validate.py enforces the first one)
  BANNED WORDS, hard error: load-bearing, honest, authentic, inauthentic, purist,
  tourist, real wings, true wings, proper wings, the real thing, sacrilege. A quotation
  in quotation marks is exempt, so quote and attribute instead of asserting.
  Wings run on argument — Buffalo against Rochester, flat against drum, bone-in against
  boneless, blue cheese against ranch. REPORT every argument. JOIN none of them. Chains,
  frozen bags, air fryers and party packs get described the same way a bar with a
  forty-year fryer does.
  Write short. Lead with a verb. Cut "the" and "what" wherever the sentence survives it.
  Fewer adjectives, more verbs. Contractions are fine. Define every non-obvious word in a
  clause at first use. No sentence that announces the next sentence. No closing summary.
  Prices carry their date.

KIN IS THE SITE'S SPINE
  Each kin entry says what the other node IS to this one, in one sentence a reader
  standing on this page would want. Not "see also". The other record answers in its own
  words, so write yours from here. Link ONLY to ids on the cast list in AUTHORING.txt.
  Aim for 5-12 kin on a rich record, 3-6 on a small one. Reach across batches freely —
  the other agent is writing that record right now.

PLACES
  geo from OpenStreetMap where data/harvest/osm-places.json has the row (grep it by name),
  otherwise the street address geocoded by hand with precision:"street" or "town".
  tags[] EVERY tag names its evidence. Ownership and welcome tags (black-owned,
  woman-owned, lgbtq-friendly) come only from the owner's own words, a press profile
  naming the owner, or a public directory. Never from a name, a photograph or a guess.
  The validator refuses tier tradition or inference on a tag.
  hours: THREE STATES, NEVER TWO — open, closed, and nobody published it. A day in
  neither list is unknown. Guessing a closure is worse than a blank.
  recognitions[]: `by` from data/vocab/recognizers.json, one per recognizer per place.

PICTURES
  images[] stays empty. Put candidate Wikimedia Commons file titles in
  x_commons_files: ["File:...jpg"] and a later pass fetches them with their licences.

FINISH BY
  running, from the repo root:  BUILD_DRAFT=1 python3 tools/validate.py
  Fix every ERROR that names one of YOUR records. Kin-target warnings for records other
  agents are still writing are expected and fine.
  Then report: how many records you wrote, which cast-list ids you could NOT source and
  why, and any fact you had to hedge.
